# Simple Interest Calculator

## Description
A simple interest calculator that calculates the simple interest
and total amount based on the principal amount, rate of interest,
and time period.

## Formula

Simple Interest = (P × R × T) / 100

Where:
- P = Principal Amount
- R = Rate of Interest
- T = Time in Years

## Example

Principal = ₹10,000
Rate = 5%
Time = 2 years

Simple Interest = (10000 × 5 × 2) / 100

Simple Interest = ₹1,000

Total Amount = ₹11,000
